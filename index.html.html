What you're signing up for

BuildIt runs for 24 straight hours. You show up Saturday morning with an idea or a blank page, form or join a team, and by Sunday afternoon you're demoing something real in front of judges.

There's no set theme and no restriction on tech stack — web apps, hardware hacks, games, ML experiments, browser extensions, anything you can finish in a day. We care more about whether you actually built it than how ambitious it sounds on paper.

Mentors from the club and a few local engineers will be around all night if you get stuck. Food, coffee, and a place to nap are covered.
